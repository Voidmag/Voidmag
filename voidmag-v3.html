Ben UFO:
The Unexpected

Placeholder Image. A DJ mixer in a club environment.

Ben Thomson, operating under the alias Ben UFO, is a British DJ and co-founder of the independent record label Hessle Audio. Emerging in the late 2000s alongside frequent collaborators Pearson Sound and Pangaea, Thomson established a reputation built squarely on selection and technical mixing ability.

Unlike the majority of contemporary touring DJs, Thomson does not produce his own music. His career path runs counter to the modern electronic music industry standard, which often requires artists to release original tracks to secure international bookings and festival slots.

His DJ sets are recognized for their stylistic breadth. A typical set often bridges gaps between distinct regional and historical scenes, integrating UK bass, garage, grime, dubstep, house, and propulsive techno. This approach prioritizes the historical lineage of dance music while simultaneously highlighting emerging and experimental producers.

"He is one of the few DJs out there who can genuinely claim to have shaped the sound of contemporary dance music without ever releasing a record."
Placeholder Image. Electronic music event atmosphere.

As detailed in a Mixmag profile profiling his ongoing influence, Thomson’s enduring appeal lies in his deep archival knowledge and ability to read a room without resorting to predictable formulas. His work with Hessle Audio continues to act as a crucial platform for experimental club music.

In a landscape increasingly dominated by easily categorized sub-genres and algorithmic playlists, Ben UFO’s methodology remains firmly rooted in the physical reality of club culture and the craft of curating records.
